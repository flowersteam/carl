CARL is a goal-conditioned reinforcement learning agent that steers Lenia solitons towards a target direction (blue arrow). It never moves the soliton directly: on every step it picks one spot on the board and either injects mass there (green disc) or erases it (red disc), over a radius of 7 cells — the soliton's own dynamics turn that nudge into motion. Each disc fades out over the following moment, so the fading trail is CARL's recent history of interventions. Use arrow keys or click anywhere on the maze to change CARL's goal. Try to navigate the soliton towards the blue circle. Switch what a click does with the click buttons above to make the same nudges yourself — click or drag on the board to add or remove mass. Your discs are drawn with a dashed ring; the arrow keys keep steering CARL either way.
